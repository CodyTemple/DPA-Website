4-Day Curriculum

What Happens Each Day

Every session opens with warm-ups and skill demos, closes with full-court games. Here's the breakdown.

Day 1 · Monday
Dinking & Footwork
🦶

Control, balance, and consistency at the kitchen line — the foundation everything else is built on.

Dinking fundamentals — forehand & backhand
Crosscourt dink games & partner drills
Footwork races & agility drills
Steal the Bacon · Hula Hoop Road · Up/Down Card Game
Team dinking competitions + full-court play
Soft HandsPaddle ControlQuick FeetPositioning
Day 2 · Tuesday
Punch & Roll Volleys
⚡

Fast reactions, strong net positioning, and confident volley play. This is where points are won.

Punch volley instruction & coach-fed drills
Roll volley technique & rally competitions
Hit the Box · Land, Sea, Air
Live Ball games & team challenges
Full pickleball match play
Quick ReactionsVolley ControlNet PlayCommunication
Day 3 · Wednesday
Serving & Returning
🎯

Serving with purpose, returning with consistency. Two shots that set the tone for every single point.

Serving technique & target practice
Return of serve drills & accuracy games
Hungry Hippos · Landmines · Three Rivers
Mini matches & King/Queen of the Court
Team games & extended match play
Serve ConsistencyReturn PlacementKitchen MovementMatch Confidence
Day 4 · Thursday — Tournament Day 🏆
Overheads & Strategy
🏅

Overheads, third shot drops, all the skills together — then a real round-robin tournament to end camp.

Overhead instruction & drills
Third shot drop training & moving challenges
Strategy discussion & tournament prep
Round-robin tournament play
Camp prizes & group photo 📸
