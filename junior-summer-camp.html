What It Is

Four Days of Real Pickleball

This isn't a "keep kids busy" camp. Every session has a focused theme — dinking and footwork, volleys, serving, and finally tournament-style play. Kids leave with real fundamentals and actual game experience.

Camp is held at a private indoor facility in Cave Creek — climate-controlled, no heat, no wind, no cancellations. Small group size (max 10) means every kid gets attention, real reps, and coaching that sticks.

Day 4 closes with a round-robin tournament, camp prizes, and a group photo. It's competitive enough to be exciting, and structured enough that even beginners feel successful.

Build strong pickleball fundamentals from the ground up
Improve footwork, coordination, and court awareness
Learn real strategy, positioning, and shot selection
Compete in a real tournament on the final day
Walk away with confidence and skills that transfer to open play

4-Day Curriculum

What Happens Each Day

Every session opens with warm-ups and skill demos, and closes with full-court pickleball play. Here's what each day focuses on.

Day 1 · Monday
Dinking & Footwork

Control, balance, movement, and consistency at the kitchen line. The foundation everything else is built on.

Activities
Dinking fundamentals — forehand & backhand
Crosscourt dink games & partner drills
Footwork races & agility drills
Steal the Bacon · Hula Hoop Road · Up/Down Card Game
Team dinking competitions & full-court play
Soft Hands Paddle Control Quick Feet Court Positioning
Day 2 · Tuesday
Punch Volleys & Roll Volleys

Fast reactions, strong positioning, and confident net play. This is where games are won.

Activities
Punch volley instruction & coach-fed drills
Roll volley technique & rally competitions
Hit the Box · Land, Sea, Air
Live Ball games & team challenges
Regular pickleball match play
Quick Reactions Paddle Positioning Volley Control Communication
Day 3 · Wednesday
Serving & Returning

Serving with purpose and returning with consistency. Two shots that set the tone for every single point.

Activities
Serving technique & target practice
Return of serve drills & accuracy games
Hungry Hippos · Landmines · Three Rivers
Mini matches & King/Queen of the Court
Team games & extended match play
Serve Consistency Return Placement Kitchen Movement Match Confidence
Day 4 · Thursday
Tournament Day

Overheads, third shot drops, and putting everything together in a real round-robin tournament. The big finish.

Activities
Overhead instruction & drills
Third shot drop training & moving drop challenges
Strategy discussions & tournament prep
Round-robin tournament play
Camp prizes & group photo 🏆
Court Awareness Team Communication Shot Selection Competitive Play
